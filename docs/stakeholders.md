# Stakeholders and Needs

Problem and scope from Milestone 1.

## Problem

Student clubs at SDU struggle with event promotion and coordination because announcements are
fragmented across Instagram, Telegram and WhatsApp. Students miss events because of information
overload, while club leaders lack a reliable way to manage registrations.

## Intended users

- **Students:** browse events, join clubs, register for activities and receive notifications.
- **Club organizers:** create and update event listings and manage club membership.
- **System administrator:** manage access rights, club accounts and platform settings.

## Scope

**The system will cover**
- Centralized event discovery with search and category filtering.
- Club profiles and member-roster management.
- Event reminders and announcement notifications.

**The system will not cover**
- Paid ticketing or online payment processing.
- Physical venue-security integration such as locks or turnstiles.
- Academic scheduling or classroom booking for official lectures.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Important needs |
|---|---|---|
| SDU students | Students who want to participate in campus life. | Find upcoming events in one place; register quickly and receive useful reminders. |
| Club organizers | Student leaders who create and host events. | Publish or edit event details; see and manage registered participants. |
| Club members | Students who follow or belong to specific clubs. | Join preferred clubs; get notifications about their new events. |
| System administrator | Person responsible for platform access and operation. | Manage roles and organizer accounts; keep the platform configured and usable. |
